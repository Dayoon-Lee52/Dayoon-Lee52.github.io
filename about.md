Hi! I am a Ph.D. student in Statistics at Ewha Womens University.

My research focuses on sufficient dimension reduction,
semi-supervised learning, and nonparametric statistics.

Currently, I am working on:

- Semi-supervised principal fitted components
- Deconvolution density estimation
- Statistical methods for mixed Euclidean and hyperspherical data

My goal is to develop statistically principled methods
that remain effective in modern data settings.

## Research Interests

- Sufficient dimension reduction
- Semi-supervised learning
- Nonparametric density estimation
- Measurement error models
- High-dimensional statistics
